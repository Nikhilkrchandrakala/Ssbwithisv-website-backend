INSTRUCTIONS FOR THE PSYCH TEST
•Greetings of the Day.
•Today you will undergo a set of psychological tests designed to understand your natural way of thinking, 
feeling, acting, and dealing with situations. These tests are not school examinations and there are no fixed 
right or wrong answers. You need not compare yourself with others. Every individual is likely to react in 
his/her own way. 
•Do not try to project an artificial personality or write what you think the Board wants to see. The tests are 
designed to study spontaneity and consistency. Therefore, your first natural response is usually more useful 
than an overthought response.
•The Psych Test battery consists of four different tests
1.Thematic Apperception Test (TAT)
2.Word Association Test (WAT)
3.Situation Reaction Test (SRT)
4.Self Description
•Instructions for each test will be flashed on the screen for 3 minutes prior commencement of that test. 
Read the instructions carefully. They are self-explanatory. Manage your time sensibly and attempt every 
test with full involvement. Even if one response does not go well, do not carry it forward mentally into the 
next test.
•Remain calm, write legibly, and express yourself freely. We are not searching for perfection; we are trying 
to understand your personality. 
•All the best.
